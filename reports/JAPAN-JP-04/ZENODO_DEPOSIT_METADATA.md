**Description / Abstract (EN):**

An independent, documentary open-source investigation into Japan's foreign-national policy measures as of 10 October 2026. The report examines Business Manager eligibility, permanent residence and naturalisation, overstayers and immigration enforcement, food service, care, agriculture and manufacturing labour demand, labour-pathway reform, tourism, administrative fees, and Japan's future JESTA travel-authorisation system. It draws on 24 research findings and seven cross-policy synthesis conclusions. The assessment distinguishes documented government justifications from observed outcomes and preserves legal-population boundaries, denominator limitations, conditional workforce forecasts and unresolved causal claims. English and Japanese public editions are included.
