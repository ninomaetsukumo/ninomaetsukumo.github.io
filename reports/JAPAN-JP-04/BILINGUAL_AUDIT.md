## Outcome and exclusions

**Editorial result:** All 24 F categories and seven S conclusions have aligned core meaning and legal/quantitative limitations. Specific key numeric series and future implementation dates are reflected in both languages. The Japanese edition is **substantively corresponding but not verbatim**, and not all archival methodological footnotes in the expanded English F appendix are translated word for word. No claim is made that an unrelated language professional independently certified the translation.

**No unresolved contradiction identified in the material claims checked.** Any subsequent discovered mistranslation is a versioned correction, not grounds for retroactively asserting a previous version had independent certification.
