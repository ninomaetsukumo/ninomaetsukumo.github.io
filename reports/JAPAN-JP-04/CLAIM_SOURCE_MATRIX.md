## Verified in this pass / 今回確認した公的記録

- [ISA October 2026 fee change effective-date and grandfathering](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/01_00644.html): fees changed for applications accepted 1 Oct 2026 onward; earlier accepted applications follow old tariff even if approval follows that date.
- [ISA FAQ: rationale, statutory ceiling reasoning, fee reductions](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/faq/01_00650.html): official funding/cost explanation; no observed deterrence effect can be inferred from this statement.
- [ISA reduction/waiver guidance](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/10_00273.html): reduced PR amount of ¥20,000 for qualifying applicants and special application-channel handling.

## Further precision needed for a fully independently verified deposit

- PR annual e-Stat tables and selected nonpayment study denominator must be matched by statInfId, vintage, and page/record to each report number.
- Tourism spending and arrival series have different observation definitions; each must cite its specific release (monthly arrivals are not spending).
- Private credit-bureau bankruptcy counts need original issuer attribution and redistribution/licensing check.
- Each Business Manager cross-country comparison needs country/legal route and date alignment. The public record does not establish a fully normalised ¥30m threshold rationale.
- The 24 English detailed entries quote/paraphrase the archival findings more fully than the Japanese entries. The **same conclusions and material quantitative claims** are identifiable in both, but no claim of verbatim line-by-line equivalence is made.


## Additional quantified-source provenance / 定量資料の補足

- **PR applications/decisions**: The original research used annual e-Stat PR processing blocks (2020–2025) and a separately reported 2025 approval table. The linked e-Stat locator is one historical entry, **not an asserted stand-alone reproduction** of 2025 counts; the original category-by-year statInfIds must be used for numeric reconstruction.

These are the originating credit-research organisation's own figures, not government measures. No reproduction of their copyrighted charts.
- **Tourism Q2 spending**: [JTA 2024 source](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/002020212.pdf), [2025 source](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/002020048.pdf), [2026 source](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/002024946.pdf). Compare identical second-preliminary release stages.
- **JESTA law**: [Act 32 of 2026](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/508AC0000000032), distinct from the [ISA implementation/explanation page](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/01_00643.html). The 2028 target is not an already-live operation.
