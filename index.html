JAPAN — JP-04 — Foreign Nationals in Japan: Policy and Economic Evidence

A cross-policy OSINT assessment of Japan's foreign-national measures, economic exposure, administrative justifications, and the limits of available evidence.

Drawing on 24 research findings and seven integrated conclusions, the report separates selective foreign-labour recruitment from status compliance, business-entry requirements, enforcement, fees, and JESTA visitor screening. It distinguishes documented rationales from unmeasured effects and avoids generalising findings across different resident and visitor populations.

JAPAN — JP-02 — Takaichi Political-Funding Network

A public-record OSINT case study of political donations, support organisations, business/public-sector intersections, vendor relationships, and evidentiary limits in Japan.

The final report maps multiple partially connected donor and support clusters, preserves negative findings and unresolved primary-source questions, and distinguishes documented relationships from allegations. It does not establish a unified donor network, quid pro quo, circular funding, or criminal liability.

Evidence visuals show the overall case structure, donor-side map, Nara support-organisation flow, Ubusuna / Local 10,000 public-benefit chain, and the Sōkōkai / Kameoka / Veanas / PoliLab branch.

Method

Project Blackbird evidence model

Verified FactDirectly supported by strong primary documentation.
Reported InformationReported by a source but not independently established by a primary record.
Analytical JudgmentInterpretation based on established facts.
HypothesisA possible explanation that remains unproven.
Negative FindingA defined public-source search did not establish the proposed relationship.
UnknownAvailable evidence is insufficient to determine the answer.

Core rule: source ≠ claim ≠ fact ≠ conclusion.

Documented association does not imply misconduct. Absence of public evidence is not treated as evidence of concealment. Timing alone is not treated as proof of causation.
