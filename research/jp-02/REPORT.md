## Important notice

This report is an independent open-source intelligence case study. It does not determine criminal, civil, electoral, tax, regulatory or ethical liability. A donation, shared address, support-group role, friendship, public contract, public-program participation, business relationship, correction, refund, amendment or complaint does not by itself establish misconduct.

## Bottom line up front

JP-02 found a **multi-cluster political-funding environment** around Sanae Takaichi-linked organisations. The strongest evidence is documentary: political-funds reports identify donations, transfers, officers, addresses and support organisations; official and corporate records document selected public-sector and business relationships; Diet records document later scrutiny and responses.

The evidence does **not** support treating the case as one unified donor network. Several clusters converge on the same political recipient but do not otherwise connect to one another in the public record.

Principal clusters at closure:

- **Toba / Toba Coffee** — large 2024 donations and campaign support involving election strategist Shinnosuke Fujikawa.
- **Kawai / Kamunagara / Noblesse** — a longer-standing relationship plus business/public-policy history, including Ubusuna-no-Sato TOMIMOTO.
- **Kikuchi / Nara Toyota / support-association infrastructure** — long relationship with Takaichi and a formal support-group link through Tsuyoshi Kinoshita.
- **Nakayama four-company cluster** — four companies with the same listed representative, same listed address, same date and ¥2.5 million donation amount.
- **Other corporate clusters** — including two 上武建設 entries and Tenri Giken.
- **Political-support infrastructure** — Nara Soshikai, Nara Prefectural Medical Political League, Ando-linked support activity and later historical-report corrections.
- **Shin-Jidai / Sōkōkai / Kameoka / Veanas / PoliLab-related infrastructure** — real personnel/address/vendor links exist, but an improper financial loop has not been established.

## 1. Core political entity

The 2024 political-funds report for the **LDP Nara 2nd District Branch** lists Sanae Takaichi as representative and provides the principal donor record used in this case. [SRC-0001]

Nara Prefecture's index records later correction activity, so the current online version should not automatically be treated as the untouched original publication. [SRC-0002]

## 2. Direct donor clusters

### Toba / Toba Coffee

The filing records two individual donations from Hiromichi Toba totalling ¥19 million and a corporate donation from Toba Coffee. [SRC-0001]

On 9 December 2025, Takaichi stated in the Diet that the branch had received ¥10 million from a company subject to a ¥7.5 million annual limit and that ¥2.5 million was returned after the issue was identified. [SRC-0003]

Toba's own public comments also establish that he asked election strategist Shinnosuke Fujikawa to support Takaichi's 2024 LDP leadership campaign. [SRC-0010]

No specific government benefit to Toba Coffee caused by these donations or campaign support was established.

### Kawai / Kamunagara

The same filing records a ¥10 million individual donation from Noriko Kawai and a ¥30 million donation from religious corporation Kamunagara / 神奈我良. The Kamunagara row identifies Kawai in the representative field. [SRC-0001]

Kawai's relationship with Takaichi predates the 2024 donations. Public materials document a 2017 regional-revitalisation roundtable involving Takaichi, the Ando mayor and Kawai around Ubusuna-no-Sato TOMIMOTO. [SRC-0008]

### Kikuchi / Nara Toyota support ecosystem

The 2024 report for the Takaichi United Support Association lists **Isao Kikuchi (菊池攻)** as representative, **Tsuyoshi Kinoshita** as accounting officer / administrative contact and **筒井町940-1** as the address. [SRC-0005]

Independent automotive-industry reporting describes Kikuchi and Takaichi as having known each other for more than three decades and identifies Kikuchi as a long-term support-association leader. [SRC-0011]

This creates a genuine organisational bridge from a corporate-support cluster into Takaichi's formal support-office infrastructure. It does not establish improper influence.

### Nakayama four-company cluster

The official 2024 filing records four donations on **23 August 2024**:

- 三幸（株） — ¥2.5 million
- 大統（株） — ¥2.5 million
- 生奈興産（株） — ¥2.5 million
- 巳斗（株） — ¥2.5 million

All four rows list **Masateru Nakayama (中山雅照)** as representative and the same listed address. Aggregate: **¥10 million**. [SRC-0001]

This is a verified internal donor cluster. The pattern is not characterised as unlawful donation splitting or evasion without separate evidence.

Repeated searches did not establish a supported bridge from this cluster to Toba, Kawai, Kikuchi, Ando, Kinoshita, Kameoka, Veanas, Sōkōkai or Shin-Jidai.

### 上武建設

The filing records two 上武建設 donations on 23 August 2024: ¥500,000 under 上武尚宏 and ¥1.5 million under 上武建一, with different listed addresses. [SRC-0001]

Nara Prefecture records independently confirm 上武建設株式会社 as a construction company headed by 上武建一. [SRC-0009]

### Tenri Giken

The current official branch filing records **Tenri Giken — ¥2 million on 19 September 2024**, represented by 手塚明宏. [SRC-0001]

Nara Prefecture qualification records confirm Tenri Giken as a construction-consultant / surveying-sector participant. [SRC-0012]

Later reporting described a criminal complaint concerning donations by companies said to have government contracts. The complaint remains an allegation and does not establish a violation, indictment or conviction. [SRC-0013]

## 3. Donor-to-donor network testing

Repeated targeted searches did **not** establish meaningful direct links among several principal donor tracks, including:

- Toba ↔ Kawai / Kamunagara
- Toba ↔ Kikuchi / Nara Toyota
- Toba ↔ Tenri Giken
- Kikuchi / Nara Toyota ↔ Kawai / Noblesse
- Kikuchi / Nara Toyota ↔ Tenri Giken
- Nakayama cluster ↔ Toba / Kawai / Kikuchi / Ando / Kinoshita / Kameoka
- 上武建設 ↔ Kawai / Noblesse
- Tenri Giken ↔ Branch B / C / D nodes

These are negative findings, not proof that no private relationship exists.

## 4. Political-support organisation branch

A 2023 Nara Soshikai filing records a ¥1 million contribution from the Nara Prefectural Medical Political League on 12 April 2023 and a ¥1 million contribution from Nara Soshikai to a Takaichi support group on 28 April 2023. Additional transfers bring the reported annual Soshikai-to-support-group total to ¥2.028 million. [SRC-0006; SRC-0007]

These filings establish transactions, not identity of funds, pass-through intent, concealment or unlawful coordination.

The professional Nara Medical Association and the political Nara Prefectural Medical Political League are treated as distinct entities.

## 5. Kawai / Noblesse / Ubusuna public-sector branch

Official and institutional materials confirm FY2015 use of the MIC Local 10,000 Project for Ubusuna-no-Sato TOMIMOTO and later operation as accommodation, restaurant and cultural-experience facilities. [SRC-0008]

Ando Town records document historical support for the former museum, later municipal land use for parking and later planning materials in which broader town-wide business and employment creation remained an ongoing policy challenge. [SRC-0008]

JP-02 did **not** establish that Takaichi personally selected the project, directed the award or that the 2024 donations were consideration for earlier public support.

The original application, exact grant / finance package and comparable KPI series were not recovered. This creates an **evaluation gap**, not evidence of concealment.

## 6. Sōkōkai / Kameoka / Veanas

The 2024 Sōkōkai filing identifies Hikaru Kajii as representative, Hirokazu Kameoka as accounting officer / administrative contact and a ¥200,000 contribution from the LDP Nara 2nd District Branch on 10 September 2024. [SRC-0004]

Corporate-registry aggregators identify Kameoka as representative member of Veanas and report the same 筒井町940-1 address. A direct registry extract remains outstanding.

Working path:

`Takaichi branch → Sōkōkai → Kameoka → Veanas`

This establishes personnel / organisational proximity, not improper control.

## 7. Shin-Jidai / Kinoshita / PoliLab

The support-association filing places Kinoshita in a formal accounting / administrative role at 筒井町940-1. [SRC-0005]

Secondary reporting states that Veanas used PoliLab for merchandise sales / fulfilment and that Shin-Jidai paid a PoliLab entity about ¥33.3 million in 2024 for advertising-related work. [SRC-0014]

JP-02 has not independently confirmed the exact PoliLab legal entity and primary payment row. Circular funding, private enrichment or self-dealing are therefore **not established**.

## 8. External political / religious context

Targeted searches did not establish a sufficiently supported donor-side or organisational bridge from the core JP-02 clusters to Sanseitō or other external political/religious actors. This material is excluded from the core public findings.

## 9. Negative findings

JP-02 did **not** establish:

- one unified donor network linking all major 2024 donor clusters;
- quid pro quo between any donation and a public benefit;
- that Takaichi personally selected or directed the Ubusuna Local 10,000 award;
- a common hidden funding source for Toba and Kawai / Kamunagara;
- circular funding through Veanas / PoliLab / Shin-Jidai;
- common beneficial ownership of the Nakayama four-company cluster beyond shared representative/address evidence;
- a supported core bridge into Sanseitō or other external religious-political actors;
- criminal liability merely because a complaint was filed;
- concealment merely because a filing was corrected or amended.

## 10. Unresolved items preserved at closure

1. Original and intermediate versions of the 2024 LDP Nara 2nd District Branch report.
2. Original-versus-amended Nara Soshikai filings and supporting receipts.
3. Direct commercial-register extracts for Veanas and the Nakayama-linked companies.
4. Exact PoliLab legal-entity resolution and primary Shin-Jidai payment row.
5. Original Local 10,000 application, exact grant / financing package and KPI sheet for Ubusuna.
6. Primary property / capital records for reported Kamunagara → World Heritage transfers.
7. Primary-row verification of provisional donor leads such as Nitori Holdings before public use.

Broad discovery is closed. Reopen only if a targeted source materially changes the case.

## Final analytical assessment

JP-02 establishes a documented political-funding environment containing direct donors, formal support organisations, business groups, professional political organisations and selected public-sector interfaces.

Some nodes connect strongly. Others remain internally connected but externally isolated in the public record. The Nakayama cluster is the clearest example: four same-date, same-amount donations with the same representative and address are verified, but no supported bridge to the major B/C/D/E clusters was found.

The evidence therefore supports a **multi-cluster network model**, not a conclusion that all participants operated as one coordinated structure.
