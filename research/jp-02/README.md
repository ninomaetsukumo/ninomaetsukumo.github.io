# Project Blackbird JP-02 — Takaichi Political Funding Network

**Status:** Closed to broad discovery / publication-ready  
**Research cut-off:** 17 September 2026

Public reports:

- [English report](REPORT.md)
- [日本語レポート](REPORT_JA.md)

JP-02 is a public-record OSINT case study of political donations, support organisations, business relationships and public-sector interfaces around Sanae Takaichi-linked political organisations.

The report does not determine criminal, civil, electoral, tax, regulatory or ethical liability. A donation, relationship, public contract, shared address, correction, refund or complaint does not by itself establish misconduct.

The evidence supports a multi-cluster network model rather than one unified donor structure. Unresolved items are preserved explicitly in both language editions.
